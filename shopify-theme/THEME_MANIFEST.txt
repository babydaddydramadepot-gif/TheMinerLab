LIQUID FEATURES
=====================================

Tag Parsing:
- Extracts specs from product tags
- Format: hashrate:140TH, efficiency:21.5 J/TH
- Displays in product cards and product pages

Supported Tag Keys:
- hashrate: Mining hashrate (e.g., "140TH")
- efficiency: Power efficiency (e.g., "21.5 J/TH")
- algo: Mining algorithm (e.g., "SHA-256")
- power: Power consumption (e.g., "3010W")

Status Tags:
- in-stock: Green badge
- pre-order: Yellow badge
- Any other: Cyan badge
